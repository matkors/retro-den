Questions

Good to know.

Something else? Call or text Tim at (757) 451-9148.

How much does a lamp repair cost?

Every lamp is different, so Tim looks first and gives you a free quote before any work starts. Send a photo and he’ll tell you what’s wrong and what it costs. Reviewers often call his prices “very reasonable” and “more than fair.”

Is my lamp worth fixing?

Almost always. Most lamps need one part, like a cord, a socket or a switch, and that costs less than a new lamp. You also keep the lamp you love.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the lamp and the parts. Simple repairs are often quick: reviewers mention “ready that afternoon” and “repair done while we waited.”

Do you pick up and deliver?

Yes, for a small fee in Virginia Beach, Norfolk and within 15 miles.

Can you fix antique, vintage and Tiffany-style lamps?

Yes. Heirlooms are restored carefully, keeping the original look. Customers have trusted Tim with great-grandmothers’ lamps, Tiffany-style lamps and a vintage Capodimonte lamp.

Do you fix floor lamps and chandeliers?

Yes, big ones too: floor lamps, swag and hanging lamps, and chandeliers.

Can you turn my ___ into a lamp?

Very likely. Tim has turned a saxophone, a beer tap handle, an old camera and a wooden base someone’s dad made into lamps. Send a photo of what you have.
